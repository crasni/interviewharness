# One human-like interviewer before personas

BLUEPRINT §3.4 planned interviewer personas as skill files with personality knobs (patience, talkativeness, strictness). We're building one well-defined, human-like good interviewer first: quiet unless speaking helps, probes reasoning, frames the round, adapts to the candidate. A persona as a "personality" isn't what makes a mock interview useful; behaving like a real good interviewer is. Personas may come back in a later version, as variations on that baseline.
