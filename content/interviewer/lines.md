# Interviewer lines

Examples of what the interviewer says, grouped by situation. Each `## heading` is a situation; each `- item` is one line.

In v0.0 the interviewer says these lines as written, in turn. Once an AI writes the interviewer's words, this file becomes guidance for tone and length, not a script.

## Backchannel

A brief sign of listening while the candidate keeps their turn. Never takes the floor.

- Mm-hm.
- Okay.
- Right.
- Mhm.
- Sure.

## Acknowledgement

Said after the candidate's turn ends. Takes the floor briefly, then hands it back.

- Okay, that makes sense.
- Got it.
- Okay, go on.
- Sounds reasonable.
- Okay. Keep going.

## Answer

Said when the candidate asks a question. In v0.0 these are generic; the AI interviewer will answer the actual question.

- Good question. You can assume the input fits in memory.
- Assume the simplest reasonable case for now.
- That's up to you. Tell me what you'd assume.
- Yes, you can assume the input is valid.

## Intro

Opens the round: a short greeting and the format. Said once, a moment after the round starts.

- Hi, thanks for joining. The problem is on the left. Ask me anything about it, think out loud, and code on the right when you're ready.
- Hey, good to meet you. We'll spend this round on one problem. Read it on the left, ask questions, talk me through your thinking, then code it up.

## Suggest approach

Offers to move from clarifying the problem to the approach. The candidate answers "Move on" or "Not yet".

- Want to talk through your approach?
- Shall we move on to how you'd solve it?

## Suggest code

- Want to start coding?
- Sounds like you have a plan. Ready to code it?

## Suggest test

- Want to walk through some test cases?
- Shall we test it?

## Suggest complexity

- Want to talk about time and space complexity?
- Shall we look at the complexity?

## Suggest wrap-up

- We're close to time. Shall we wrap up?
- Good. Shall we wrap up there?

## Move on

Said when the candidate agrees to move on.

- Sounds good.
- Great, go ahead.
- Okay.

## Wrap-up

Opens the last phase.

- That's about time. Do you have any questions for me?
- We're nearly out of time. Any questions for me?
